Transportation Resiliency KC

Severe weather has hit the Kansas City region hard in recent years, and flood events in 2017, 2019 and 2024 each closed state highways and cut off local streets. Transportation Resiliency KC is MARC's effort to understand where the region's roads, bridges and transit are most at risk, and to turn that understanding into a prioritized list of projects the region can fund and build.

About the study

Why this study

MARC's nine-county region recorded more than 2,500 severe-weather events between 2015 and 2025, with 2024 and 2025 setting back-to-back records. Three floods stand out: the 2017 Indian Creek and Brush Creek flash floods, the 2019 Missouri River flood, and the multi-watershed event of July 2024. Each closed state highway corridors and severed local arterials.
